03 2026

HJM–Lifted Heston for Energy Markets — Historical Calibration

Python · NumPy · SciPy · CVXPY · EEX Data

  • M1 research project (ENSIIE / Paris-Saclay, supervised by Prof. Sergio Pulido) on the HJM–Lifted Heston model for energy markets, following Abi Jaber et al. (2025).
  • Led the historical calibration: derived analytic covariances of rolling futures log-returns under a Nelson–Siegel (Level–Slope–Curvature) multifactor volatility parametrisation, reformulated as a linear conic programme (SDP + SOCP) solved via CVXPY.
  • Calibrated a 1L-1S-1C model on 494 days of EEX Phelix-DE power futures; achieved median covariance error of 3.4%, recovering the Samuelson effect from M1 (62%/yr) to Cal1 (6.7%/yr).
  • Extended the model with a jump component in instantaneous forward dynamics; derived the Riccati system preserving the affine exponential transform structure on the Kemna–Vorst geometric proxy.
